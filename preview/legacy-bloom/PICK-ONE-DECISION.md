DECISION — Danny agrees with Jett: PICK ONE
After the narrator's voice and the beautiful flower, the beginning introduces the whole system. Then the customer picks one companion — you either get the chick or you get the dude. Gus or June. One companion, one customer. Not both in the Bench together, not a panel.

Why pick one, in Danny's and Jett's words:
- One steady friend you know, not two voices talking over each other. Especially for people rebuilding their lives.
- One memory thread, one relationship. The companion remembers what you actually said — the Lowe's stones, the yard, the traffic — and brings it back.
- Both together would run two live models per turn: roughly double the backend cost, double the data, double the latency, more to go wrong, memory gets messy — who remembers what?
- Pick one runs lighter, cheaper, simpler, and it matches Danny's own rule: one companion, one customer.

THE OPENING — in order
1. The narrator's voice — the over-voice at the entrance — gets you in.
2. The beautiful flower — the opening is the flower. You scroll through the beginning once until you envelop the character companion relationship.
3. The beginning introduces the whole system and the thoughts of the atmospheres — all four.
4. You pick one atmosphere that is calming to you, the one you are using at the time.
5. You pick your companion — Gus or June — and that is yours.

THE FOUR ATMOSPHERES — and why four
Flower Garden, Ocean Drift, Zen Garden, Celestial.
The reason Danny did four atmospheres is because you could have different ones for different things. The Flower Garden could be your tranquil one — just make sure I drink five cups of water a day, etc.
Habits are put in BY the customer, not already stacked. The customer creates their habit. It could be one per atmosphere, it could be four per atmosphere.
You pick a stack of your habits, or your want-to-do lists, or things you want consistent feedback on.
General reminders: day, noon, and night. All of that changes the scene of each of the atmospheres.
Each atmosphere has quiet, calming background noise.
The AI comforts, evaluates, educates, evolves — the three E's. It remembers, it stats it up, it visually displays it in the active interactive companion engine.

THE BENCH — Gus / June vent part
The Bench is for venting. You walk in, Gus / June is there wiping the counter: "Do you leave it all here, or we can talk you through it?"
Small reply, nothing telling you what to do.
You vent, you hit stop or you go silent 8-10 seconds, then they reply with a prompt — not cushy empathy, no "feel better." They tell you how to use that anger and strengthen it toward a goal in your rant.
They come back with a conversational piece, not an answer, not "sorry to hear that." They show they heard you.
They decipher from their memory of your conversations, and give a substantial, specific, memory-grounded reply — banter.
Leave it all at the Bench. Words dissipate into the Cheshire Cat and steam. You feel better, you leave. It automatically remembers.
Three nudges a day: morning, noon, and evening. It does not remind you, it gives you a nudge.

THE LADY PART — June
June is the same exact engine, same everything, just June's voice doing the atmospheres and the flowers. Not all women are going to want some run-down Gus. That is why Danny created June.
Gus 2.1 is the live Gus — from the Gem, talks forever, different lines, agile, can go find stuff online. The recordings are for Gumroad only, NOT the app Gus.
June's real voice must welcome you in at the start. No robotic TTS. No instructions telling you to look around and push things.
The flower must breathe and vary, never a flat 8-second loop. Gus must not be a 3-8 second loop looking off to the edge.

THE REPLY RULE
A reply is NOT a marked clip card off a stack that repeats. It must acknowledge, remember, recite, rephrase, rethink, educate, and involve, and send out good best-friend feelings.

LEAF TUG = SETTINGS = DECIPHER
Tug a leaf, it opens Settings. Settings creates the Decipher effect — when someone is talking, the AI can pre-decipher if they are upset, so it knows, not just re-questions. That is where the Cipher came from.

FUTURE — not now
We can create an in-store / in-app pay pack in the future, or a Gus 3.0 expansive pack. That is later, not now. Do not build it yet.
